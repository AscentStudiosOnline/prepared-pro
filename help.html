How to use Prepared Pro

Plan it, make it, deliver it

Appearance

When Prepared Pro opens: you can have it start fresh on My Day (the usual), ask you each time, or open where you left off. Choose your setup for my kind of work is also here. Under Appearance you can choose light, dark or automatic, a larger text size, one of four type styles and one of fourteen color themes named after drinks: Green tea, Blueberry, Grape juice, Coffee, Cream, Banana smoothie, Peach tea, Orange soda, Cherry cola, Strawberry milk, Whiskey, Seltzer, Blue lagoon and Milk and espresso (black and white). A theme tints the whole app, including the opening screen. The clock can be shown top right or bottom right, or hidden. The look settings (theme, text size, type style and clock) are for this device only and are not part of a backup, because each screen needs its own. Under Left sidebar buttons you can show the main buttons in the usual order, A to Z, most used first, or your own order with Up and Down. Your choice and your own order are saved with your backup, so they come back when you restore on another device. The Most used count stays on each device. Show only the main buttons hides everything except My Day, Projects, Calendar, Notebook and Search, so a new person sees less at first. That choice, the extra buttons a setup added, and the setup you chose go with your backups and syncs, so your devices show the same sidebar. How the app opens, light or dark and text size stay on each device. Nothing is deleted, and you can turn it off at any time.

The Projects, Series and Personal groups in the left column start closed each time you open Prepared Pro, so the menu looks clean. Tap a group to open it.

Ask my AI (optional)

Prepared Pro does not contain an AI and never sends your work anywhere. If you use an AI of your own, the Ask my AI button builds a clear request from your project, and Copy for my AI puts it on your clipboard so you can paste it into the AI you choose. Only the parts you select are included.

Prompt maker: on a project's Overview, tap Prompt maker. Every kind of project has its own ready-made requests. For a podcast episode they include the roll-in (cold open and intro), the roll-out (wrap-up and sign-off), hook ideas, titles, show notes and promo clips; for an inspection, a toolbox talk and clear findings wording; for an interview, a practice interview and a thank-you email; and so on. Step 1 is what you need. Step 2 is the basics, such as the name of the show, who it is for and the tone you like. You fill them in once. When the project is in a series, every project in the series shares them. Step 3 is anything special about this one. Then copy the result into the AI you like. Under More choices you can ask for a prompt for an AI video maker such as Grok, Sora, Runway or Veo, choose its shape and length, or have your AI improve the request first. When the answer comes back, paste it in step 5 and it goes into your project with a [cue] label.

Supporters and letters: in Contacts, give each person a category (Donor, Monthly partner, Prospect, Volunteer, Prayer partner, Board, Church or partner organization), set how often you want to be in touch, and press Contacted today. People who are due show on My Day and in the Due to contact list. Record donations in a person's Donations section. You can export a mailing list of who is shown. Letters (in the side list, and on each project's Overview): start from a sample or write your own. Words in double braces, like {{First name}}, fill in for each person; send one letter to one person, a group or a category, with a preview that warns you about anything missing. Add a letterhead (logo, address lines, footer), then print or save as PDF, make a Word file, copy as text, or print mailing labels. Polish with my AI, Fine-tune with my AI and Draft the follow-up with my AI make a ready request that you copy into your own AI; nothing is sent from the app. Donation thank-you wording is a starting point, so have your accountant confirm it. Notebook folders can hold subfolders (up to three levels): open a folder and choose New subfolder in here. Under any notes box, See how it will look shows your indents as they will print. Finances (in the side list) lists every donation by person, fund and year, and Letters can fill in {{Total given this year}}. Every project has a Costs tile: add areas such as Building A, then add entries for lumber, labor and other costs, with a budget and totals per area. Backup and restore: name the computer so you can tell your backups apart, and add an optional password. A forgotten password cannot be recovered. Contact history: press Log a call (in My Day or on a contact) to record a phone call, visit, video call, meeting, letter, email or text. Letters you send, and meetings you mark Held that name the person or group, log themselves. Use the Not contacted filter to see who you have not reached in 30 to 365 days. Catalog: in the side list, keep every product, book, film, decoration or course, tap each step as it is finished, then On the website, On sale and Promoted, and keep its page address. Podcast: choose Podcast in New series to track episodes with planned and aired dates and a list for show notes. Letters: Save as my template adds a letter to My templates. Categories now include Customer, Wholesale, VIP and Lead. Your license works on every device you own and covers this version and its minor updates; a major new version is offered to you at a discount.

Backup and restore

Because your work lives on this device, make a backup now and then. Choose Backup and restore, then Save a backup file. Keep it somewhere safe, such as your cloud storage or an email to yourself.

To restore, choose the same screen and pick your file. You can Add the backup's projects to what you have, or Replace everything with the backup. The left column reminds you when it has been a while.

Clearing the browser's website data on your device erases your projects, so back up first.

The Send or save button appears only in browsers that can open the share sheet (Safari, and your iPhone or iPad). In Chrome on a Mac, press Save a backup file, then in Finder right-click the file and choose Share, then AirDrop.

In Chrome and Edge, Save to a folder puts a dated backup file straight into a folder you choose once, such as your Dropbox folder, a USB drive or a network drive. Later backups go to the same folder; use Change folder to pick another. If you set a password, the file in that folder is locked with it.

Sync. When you open a backup file from another of your devices, a Sync button appears beside Add and Replace. Sync keeps the newest change to each project, contact, note and other item, adds anything new, and carries over things you deleted, so work on two devices is combined instead of overwritten. If you change something on one device and delete it on the other, the newer action wins. Your theme, text size, clock position, maps app and other settings for each device stay as they are. Press Undo right after to put everything back as it was. Replace everything still makes this device match the file exactly. Sync works from backups made in version 3.64 or later.

Sync Now! Press Sync Now! at the top of Backup and restore to bring two of your own devices up to date with each other without saving a file. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi and open Prepared Pro on both. On the first one press Show my code. On the second press Scan their code and point its camera at the first screen. The second device then shows a code of its own. On the first press Scan their reply and point its camera at that. The two connect directly and combine their changes, pictures included, in the same careful way as Sync. Nothing goes through the internet or to anyone else. If a camera will not work, use Copy code and paste the code into the other device. If the two are on different networks, tick the box on the first screen. Press Undo on the Done screen if you want to put this device back as it was. Both devices need version 3.66 or later and must each have been opened once on that version.

More ways to sync: your own Dropbox (optional). You do not need Dropbox. It helps when your devices are not together. Open Backup and restore, then Sync Now!, then More ways to sync. Step 1: on dropbox.com/developers/apps press Create app, choose Scoped access and App folder, and name it anything. Step 2: on its Permissions tab tick files.content.write and files.content.read, then press Submit at the bottom. Step 3: on its Settings tab copy the App key, not the App secret. Step 4: in Prepared Pro paste the App key, make up a passphrase and write it on paper, then press Connect to Dropbox. On the Dropbox page press Continue and Allow, copy the code it shows, and paste it back into Prepared Pro. Step 5: press Sync with Dropbox now. On each other device, use the same App key and the same passphrase. Everything is locked with your passphrase before it goes to Dropbox, so only your devices can read it, and if you forget the passphrase nobody can recover the files. Tick Sync automatically if you want this device to sync when you open the app and a minute after you stop making changes. Nothing is deleted unless it was really deleted on one of your devices, and Undo is offered after each sync.

Calculator and clock size

Press Calculator in the left bar. Basic works with the keys or your keyboard and keeps a tape you can copy. Price and tax finds the total with discount, tax and tip, and tells you which size is the better buy. Estimate adds up lines (hours or items times the price each) with contingency, markup and tax; start from IT project, Publication, Construction or Video shoot. Building figures area, boxes or gallons with waste, and concrete in cubic yards. Print run shows your cost per copy, profit and break-even. Your numbers are remembered on this device. In Settings you can make the corner clock Small, Medium or Large.

Above your project list, Sort projects lets you choose Due soon, Last opened, Name A to Z, Newest first or Oldest first. Each device remembers its choice. In Backup and restore, press Send or save a backup to AirDrop it, put it in Dropbox or Files, or email it. Shared project files and changes use the same share sheet. On My Day, press Arrange My Day, then drag the Move bars to put each panel where you want it, even into another column. Each device keeps its own arrangement. Importing a Google Contacts CSV now brings in job titles, labels as groups, and extra phones and emails. In a contact, use More phone numbers and email addresses to keep a work phone, a home email and others. They show on the contact, are searched, and go into exports. When you make a group in Contacts, you can add a new person right in the group box. They are saved in Contacts too.

Calendar and reminders

The dots and small labels on the calendar take the color of the project they belong to: red when the project is behind schedule, amber when it is falling behind, green when it is on track or ready, and grey when it has no delivery date or is not part of a project. The same words appear when you hold or tap a day, and in the list for that day, so you never depend on color alone. The project Overview shows a This week and overdue box with everything due in the next seven days or already late, from all your projects. Tap a row to open that project. Calendar and reminders (in the left column) makes a calendar file of your due dates. Pick how early you want a reminder. Open the file and your calendar app adds the events. Projects marked Delivered are left out. The Calendar window has two views. List shows what is coming up in order; Month shows a calendar page with each delivery date, event and trip on its day (red is a delivery date, green an event, shoot or booking, gold travel). Tap a day to see its items, and tap an item to open its project.

If your device will not download the file directly, send it to yourself by email and open it from there.

To add an appointment, use Add something on My Day (choose Appointment and set the time), or open a day in the calendar and tap Add an appointment on this day. Times are optional. Your to-dos, follow-ups and appointments show on their day in the calendar and on the calendar download; tap one to go to My Day.

Checklist

The Checklist page lists everything you need to finish, grouped by category. On a Writing and Publishing project, Add blog post checks adds a Blog post group: headlines, keyword, description, image with alt text, links, scheduling and sharing. Tap an item to change it from empty to started to done. Add a note to any item, or add your own items.

You can edit the wording of any item, mark it required or optional, and remove what does not apply. Your changes belong to that project only.

Contacts

Contacts (in the left column) is one address book for every project: name, role, organization, phone, email, address, category and notes. Search by anything you remember, or filter by category. Tap a phone number or email to call or write. People can have a small photo. In Contacts or on a People page, open someone and choose Add a photo; the picture is cropped square and shrunk to a thumbnail, so it takes almost no room. Without a photo, a circle with their initials is shown. Photos stay on this device, are part of your backup, are carried when you add someone from Contacts or save them to Contacts, travel in the .vcf contacts file, and print in the whole-project report when Show people's photos is ticked. A person on a project with no photo of their own shows the photo of the contact with the same email or name, even if the photo was added to Contacts later.

Anyone you add to a project's People page, and each place's contact person, is also saved here for you. On a film project's People page, Add from Contacts puts the people you choose on that project, and Save to Contacts keeps the people you typed in there for next time. You can save your Contacts as a file that your phone or computer contacts app can open (.vcf), as a spreadsheet, or copy them as text. Contacts are part of your backup.

Import contacts: on the Contacts page press Import contacts and choose a spreadsheet (.csv) or a contacts file (.vcf) from Google, Outlook, Apple or another program. Prepared Pro matches the columns it recognizes (name, first and last name, role, organization, phone, email, address, category, groups and notes), and you can change any match. A list shows who was found, with anyone already in your Contacts left out. Untick anyone you do not want, then press Import. Undo is offered right afterward. A Groups column (names separated by semicolons) puts people into those groups and makes any that are missing. Imported people come in without ways to meet, which you can add afterward. The spreadsheet you save from Contacts has the same columns, so you can move your contacts between devices.

In Finances, Record a donation also lets you pick A new person, who is saved in Contacts at the same time.

Customize My Day and your maps app

Two more panels can be shown. Daily routines are short checklists that clear themselves every morning, such as Before I speak or Start of the day. Make them from the three dots on My Day, then Daily routines. Journal is a small panel with a button that opens today's journal entry in your Notebook. It starts hidden, so turn it on here. On My Day, press Customize My Day. Tick the panels you want to see, move them up or down, or start from a quick choice: Everything, Work mix, Personal mix or Simple. In the same box, choose your maps app. Each device keeps its own choice, so your Mac can use Apple Maps while your phone uses Google Maps (Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze or OpenStreetMap). Every Directions and Map link then opens in that app.

On a wide screen you can also choose Three columns, which spreads the panels out so more fit without scrolling. This is kept on this device too.

Looks. Press Appearance and choose Colors: Match my device, Light, Dark, Corporate (clean greys), High contrast (black on white), Sepia (soft paper) or Midnight (dark blue). In Choose your setup, tick the box at the bottom if you also want the look suggested for that setup. Nothing changes your colors unless you tick it.

Four columns. On a very wide screen, Add or remove panels lets you choose Four columns. The side panels then flow into two columns. A narrower screen simply shows its usual layout.

Collapse a panel. Each My Day panel has a small arrow at its top right. Press it to roll the panel up to just its title. It is still there, and you press the title to open it again. Each view remembers which panels are rolled up. In the ⋯ menu, Collapse all panels and Expand all panels do it for the whole screen. Panels that have something late show a red "overdue" tag. A collapsed panel with late items turns red, and an open one gets a red glow. The red goes away when the late items are done.

Every page can be a panel. Press the three dots on My Day, then Add or remove panels. Besides your to-dos and calendar, you can now show Meetings coming up, Recent notes, Projects, Series, Contacts, Letters, Finances, Catalog, a Search Everything box, and Quick links to every page. They start hidden, so tick the ones you want. Finances shows only a short summary, but if others can see your screen, leave it off.

One layout for each way you work. Set up My Day the way you like it for Home, then press the three dots and Save this layout as a new view. Name it, such as Office or Writing, and press Add view. The new view is a copy, so change only what differs. Switch between views with the buttons under My Day.

Views. My Day can have several views, such as Home and Work. In Customize My Day, type a name under New view and press Add view. It starts as a copy of the view you are in, so you only change what differs. Once you have two or more views, their names appear as buttons under My Day, and you press one to switch. Each view keeps its own panels, order, columns of panels and hidden panels. Views are saved with your backup and come back when you restore on another device. Which view is showing is remembered on each device.

Your own panels. In Customize My Day, under Add to this view, pick a project, a series, a Notebook folder, a contact group or a contact category and press Add this panel. A project panel shows what is coming up and its open to-dos. A series panel lists its projects and what is coming up. A Notebook folder panel lists the newest notes in that folder and all of its sub-folders, with New note here and Open in Notebook buttons. A group or category panel lists those people with their phone and email and a Contacted today button. Press Remove beside a panel to take it off that view. Nothing is deleted from your projects or contacts.

When a My Day item has a Project box, choose A new project to make one on the spot and link it. Who it involves can hold several people: pick them one after another from your contacts, or type names with commas between them.

Deleting, copying and renaming projects

In the left column, press the three dots on a project, or right-click it on a computer, or press and hold it on an iPad or phone. A menu offers Open, Rename, Duplicate and Delete. On a touch screen you can also swipe a project to the left to show a red Delete button.

Delete always asks first, and for a few seconds afterward a message offers Undo. Duplicate makes a copy called "(copy)" with the dates cleared and everything else kept, including your progress. In the free version a copy counts as a project. You can also delete from Edit project, in the project menu.

Event and travel, and the Bring list

For events, enter the place, time, travel plans and contacts on the Event and travel page. The Bring list keeps track of what must go with you. You can save your usual list and start the next event from it.

Filming and shoot days

Script and Film projects include the filming pages. For any other kind of project, press Choose extras (or Go to, then Add or remove extras) and switch on Filming and shoot days.

  • People: your cast and crew with role, phone, email and call time. Tap the circle to move each person from Not asked to Asked to Confirmed, and track each release form as Needs one, Sent or Signed. Add from Contacts and Save to Contacts share names with your Contacts book.
  • Places: each location with address, contact, parking, power and permits, and your light and weather plan. A Map link opens the address.
  • Gear: your equipment list. Mark each piece as yours, rented, borrowed, to buy, or brought by the crew, and tick it off as it is packed.
  • Shots: the shots you need, so nothing is missed on the day. Mark each To shoot, Partly or Got it.
  • Schedule: when and where the work happens. Add studio bookings, setup, tapings, rehearsals and editing sessions with a date, times, the studio or place and who is needed. Tap the circle to move each from Wanted to Held to Booked. If the same studio is used twice at the same time, even by two different projects, both are flagged. Print the schedule, make a Word document or copy it as text. Booked and held times also appear in Calendar and reminders.
  • Production: set up the cameras, format, background, lights and sound, and plan the post-production (editor, delivery, music and licenses, notes). Buttons there add green screen checks and post-production checks to your Checklist. The camera and sound setup also prints on the call sheet.
  • Call sheet: set the shoot day (date, general call, wrap, weather, nearest hospital, safety notes) and Prepared Pro builds a one-page call sheet from everything above. Print it or save it as a PDF, make a Word document, or copy it as text to send.

The film checklist also includes Shoot-day checks for camera settings, a microphone test, lighting and wrap-up. On another kind of project, open Shots and press Add camera, sound and light checks.

Finances: money in, money out and who owes you

Finances is a simple record for your own use. It is not accounting software, so have your accountant confirm anything you file or send out. It has six tabs. Overview shows money in, money out, what is left over and what is owed to you for a year, split by kind and by project. Money in, Money out and Mileage list each entry. Owed to me lists unpaid invoices. Donations is the same donation record as before, with thank-you tracking and funds.

Add an entry. Press Add money in, Add an expense or Add mileage. Choose the date, amount and a kind (the list suggests common kinds, and you can type your own), who paid you or whom you paid, and the project if there is one. For mileage, type the miles and your rate per mile. Prepared Pro remembers the rate but does not choose one for you, so ask your accountant which rate to use.

Who owes me. On a Money in entry, tick Not paid yet and choose a due date. It appears under Owed to me and is not counted as money in until you press Mark paid. Turn on the Money owed to me panel in Add or remove panels on My Day, and it shows anything overdue or due within a week.

Receipt photos. On a Money in or Money out entry, press Add photos under Receipt photos to attach up to 3 pictures of a receipt or invoice. They stay on your device and go into a backup or sync with your other pictures.

Reports. Pick the year at the top of Overview. Spreadsheet (.csv) saves that year's entries, including donations, for your accountant or for tax time. Donations you already recorded count as money in automatically.

My Day. The Finances panel shows this month's money in, money out and what is left, with buttons to add an entry. If other people can see your screen, leave it off.

Private journal

Journal is a panel on My Day with a button that opens today's entry in your Notebook. You can protect it with its own passphrase. Open the Journal panel and press Protect with a passphrase. Choose a passphrase of at least 8 characters and tick the box saying you understand it cannot be recovered. Your existing journal entries are scrambled right away, and so is every new one.

You can also protect or lock the journal from the Notebook. In the Journal folder, press Protect journal, or Lock journal when it is already protected. When you write an entry in the Journal folder, the same link appears under the Folder choice.

The scrambled entries travel with Sync Now, Dropbox and backups, but they cannot be read without the passphrase, on any device. On another device, the Journal panel says Locked until you press Unlock and type the passphrase there. It locks itself after a few minutes away, when you leave the app for a minute or more, or when you press Lock now or the app Lock.

Things to know. Locked entries do not show up in Search Everything, printing or Send to Word, and their text is not copied into projects. An entry's title and tags are not scrambled, so keep them to the date. Pictures added to a journal entry are not scrambled. Nobody can recover a forgotten passphrase, so write it down somewhere safe. To go back to normal notes, press Turn off protection and type the passphrase. Undo is offered for both steps.

Formatting your notes

In an outline section, the Talking notes box has a row of buttons above it. They make your notes look good on screen, in Speaker view, when printed, and in Word.

  • Bold and Italic: select words and press the button, or type **two stars** around bold words and *one star* around italic words.
  • List makes a bulleted list and 1. List makes a numbered list. Press Enter to start the next line. Press Enter on an empty line to end the list. Numbers fix themselves if you add or remove a line.
  • In and Out, or the Tab and Shift+Tab keys, move a line in or out. You can go four levels deep. Bullets change shape and numbers change to letters as you go in. Press Escape first if you want Tab to leave the box.
  • Text pasted from Word or a web page keeps its bullets and indents.

Everything is saved as plain text, so older notes are not changed.

Free version and your license code

The free version lets you keep up to two projects. The full version removes that limit. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription.

After you buy, you receive a license code. To unlock:

  • Open Prepared Pro and press the License line at the bottom of the left column.
  • Paste your code exactly as it appears in your receipt.
  • Press Activate. The line then reads Full version unlocked, or Team edition unlocked if your code is a Team code.

The code works without an internet connection. You enter it once on each device where you use the program. Keep your receipt, because it is your proof of purchase. If you ever clear your browser data, enter the code again.

Goals, check-ins and risks

A Goals and Objectives project is for work that runs for weeks or months instead of ending on one day. Use it for a business or organization plan, a year or quarter plan, goals for an office or a team, a grant or budget year, or a personal target. Set the final date as the due date. The checklist walks you through four steps: define the goal, plan it, get agreement, and review it. The outline holds one objective per section, with the reason, the measure, the starting point and the target.

  • Check-ins: plan a check-in for each month or quarter. After you meet, write down how you are doing, what changed and what you decided. Tap the circle to move it from Planned to Held to Actions closed.
  • Risks and decisions: list what could get in the way and what must be decided, with an owner and a date. Tap the circle to move each from Open to Working on it to Resolved.
  • People holds the owners and helpers. Schedule holds review meetings. Check-in and resolve dates appear in Calendar and reminders.

On any other kind of project, press Choose extras and switch on Goals and check-ins.

Installing on your device

Prepared Pro works in your web browser and can be installed like an app.

  • iPad or iPhone: open it in Safari, press the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome, open the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Computer: open it in Chrome or Edge and press the install icon at the end of the address bar.

After the first visit it also works with no internet connection. Your projects are saved on your own device, never on our server.

Please install it to the Home Screen on iPad and iPhone. Safari can clear the saved data of a website you have not opened for about a week, but an app added to the Home Screen is kept. Clearing Safari's website data also erases your projects, so use Backup now and then. Apps that open web pages inside other apps, such as HTML viewers, are not supported. Use Safari on iPad and iPhone, and Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer.

My templates

When you have a checklist and outline you like, open the Go to menu and choose Save as template. You can start a new project from it any time. Manage or rename your templates from the New project screen.

Notebook: ideas, notes and pictures

The Today's journal entry button makes a note called Journal and today's date in a Journal folder, or opens it again if you already started it. Sizes and fonts. Select some words in a note, then choose Size (Small, Normal, Large, Extra large) or Font (Standard, Book, Clean, Typewriter) above the note box. Choose Normal or Standard to go back. In the talking notes box the same lists work on the words you have selected. Sizes and fonts show in the note, in Print and in Word documents. Emails and copied text keep the words only, and the speaker view and teleprompter keep their own sizes.

Dictate. Press Dictate beside the note box, the Add something box on My Day, or the talking notes, and speak instead of typing. The first time, Prepared Pro tells you that the sound goes to your browser's speech service (Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari, Microsoft for Edge) to be turned into words. Your notes stay on your device. Say new line or new paragraph to start a new line, and period, comma or question mark for punctuation. Press Stop dictating when you are done. Dictate does not appear in browsers that cannot do it, such as Firefox. On any phone or tablet you can also use the microphone key on the keyboard. Print. Every note has a Print button, and the note screen has Print this note, which prints what you see even before you save it.

Moving and sending notes. New note and Add a photo are at the top of the list. Tick the box beside a note, or Select all, and a bar appears: pick a folder under Move to and press Move, or press Send. Send offers the share sheet (on a phone or iPad), an email draft, a Word document with the photos, a print or PDF page, a Prepared Pro note file, or Copy the text. A note file can be opened by another Prepared Pro in Backup and restore, and the notes and photos go into their Notebook.

The Notebook is part of the full version. It is one place for ideas that do not belong to any single project yet. Press Notebook in the left column. Make folders of your own, such as Sermon ideas, Illustrations or Book two. Add notes with a title, tags and as much text as you like. In a Notebook note, what you see is what you get: press B for bold, I for italic or U for underline (or Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+U; on a Mac, Command), choose a bulleted or numbered list, and use In and Out (or Tab and Shift+Tab) to move just the line you are on. Notes you wrote before open exactly as they were. Add pictures to a note too. They are shrunk to a sensible size and kept on your device. The Notebook shows your folders on the left and the notes on the right (on a phone or small tablet, tap Folders to switch). There are four built-in views: All notes, Unfiled, Recent and Photos, which gathers every picture from every note into one gallery; tap a picture to open its note. My folders are the ones you make yourself. Project folders appear by themselves once a note is filed under a project, and projects in a series are grouped under the series name. Choose where a note lives with the Folder box when you open it. Add a photo makes a new note around a picture. Add to project files a note that has no folder under that project. If you delete a project, its notes move to Unfiled.

  • Search looks through titles, words, tags and folder names. Press a tag to see everything with that tag.
  • Add to project puts a note into a project: into the talking notes of a section you choose, or as a new checklist item. Pictures stay in the Notebook. You can undo it.
  • While you edit a section, Insert from my Notebook brings a note in, and Save these notes to my Notebook keeps what you wrote for later.
  • Rename or delete a folder from the folder list. Deleting a folder keeps its notes and moves them to Unfiled.
  • Backup and restore saves the Notebook too. Tick Include my Notebook pictures if you want the pictures in the file. The file is larger.

Everything stays on your own device. Clearing your browser's website data erases the Notebook, so back up now and then.

Outline

Each section has an Owner box for the person responsible. It offers the people on the project's People page, and you can type any name. The owner shows on the section's row, a Show list above the outline lets you see one person's sections, and the name is included when you export or print. Import script brings in a script you already wrote. Paste it, or choose a Word (.docx), text (.txt) or Markdown (.md) file. Prepared Pro splits it at headings, scene headings (INT. and EXT.), numbered or capitalized lines, or into parts of about 300 words, and shows you the sections first. Rename a section, untick one you do not want, or join two together, then press Import. Each section keeps your text in its talking notes, so Speaker view can scroll it. Press Undo right after if you change your mind. The Outline page is where you build the shape of your talk, chapter, class or script. Add a section, give it a heading, a subject, bullets and notes, and mark it empty, started or done.

Use the arrow buttons to move sections up or down. If you are stuck, the Ideas button offers questions and starting points for openings, points, illustrations and application. These are built into the program and do not use AI.

Print, Word and PowerPoint

Printing the whole project. In Export or print, the box called Include the rest of the project adds a readiness summary at the top, and after the outline the checklist with what is done and what is still missing, then each extra page your project uses: event and travel, people, places, gear, shots, schedule, review rounds, deliverables, check-ins, and risks and decisions. Untick any part you do not want. It works for Print or save as PDF, Word and Copy as text. PowerPoint slides hold the outline only.

Choose Export or print on a project. You can make a Word document, PowerPoint slides (one slide per section, with your notes in the speaker notes), a printed page or PDF, or plain text to paste elsewhere.

Three looks are offered: Clean, Classic and Bold. On an iPad or iPhone the file may open in a preview first. Use the Share button there to save it to Files or send it to another app.

The readiness meter and stages

The meter at the top of each project shows how much of the work is done. A done item counts fully, a started item counts half, and an empty item counts nothing. Items marked required count twice as much as optional ones.

Your outline can count too. If the box for outline sections is ticked in Edit project, each section counts as started once you have added something to it, and done when you mark it done.

The project moves through its stages on its own: Planning, Drafting, Polishing and Ready. You mark it Delivered yourself when it is over, and you can reopen it if you need to.

Release form

Press Release form on the People page (or on Contacts) for a blank release for anyone who appears in your work. Change the wording to suit you, add your company name, and choose whether it has lines for a parent or guardian. Keep your changes as your standard form for every project, or for this project only. Reset brings back the original wording.

Print or save as PDF, make a Word document, or copy it as text. On the People page you can also choose a person, and their name, phone and email are filled in. The form is a general starting point and is not legal advice, so have an attorney review it before you rely on it.

Series

A series groups related projects, such as the books of a study or the weeks of a course. Open Series from the left column to make one, add projects to it and put them in order with the arrow buttons. When you make a series you say what its parts are called (books, episodes, films, lessons, talks, weeks or projects), and the buttons and milestone columns use that word; films and episodes start with Scripted, Filmed, Edited, Approved, Published and Promoted. The series page shows how ready the whole set is. In the left column a series is one folder row with its combined progress and next due date; tap it to open or close the list of its projects (the three dots open the series page). The folder opens by itself when you choose one of its projects. Once you have six or more projects the list is grouped into Due soon (the next two weeks and anything late), In progress, and Delivered, which is folded away until you open it. A search box above the list finds a project by title, type, series or stage.

Each series also has an overview for the whole set: its theme, audience, source, arc, tone, notes and planned number of parts. Press Add series overview on the series page. You can print it, make a Word document or copy it as text. When you write the big picture of a project in the series, a button fills in the series theme and audience for you.

Plan a week: press Plan a week on the Series page (or from New project). Choose a template, such as Podcast episode, how many, the first date and which days, and Prepared Pro makes one project per day in one series, each with its date set. The series page then shows a Schedule and rehearsal list, every project by date with how ready it is and a Speaker view button on each row. Undo removes what it just made.

Saved lines: in any outline section, tap one of the Insert a saved line buttons to put a sponsor read, thank-you, subscribe request or other line you say again and again into your talking notes. Choose Edit my lines to change them, add your own, or bring back the starter lines. Put (round brackets) around the parts you change for each episode.

On the Series page, each series has a button to start a new project in it, and a New project button sits beside New series.

Sharing with the Team edition

The Team edition lets you send a project or a template to another person as a file. It does not sync live between people. Each person keeps their own copy.

To send a project:

  • Open the project, open the Go to menu and choose Share this project.
  • Choose Share it as it is now to send everything, including what you have marked done. Or choose Share a clean starting copy, which keeps the checklist, outline and notes but clears what is marked done or started, so the other person begins fresh.
  • Prepared Pro makes a file. Send it by email, AirDrop, Messages or any way you like.

Your Notebook notes and your Sources library are never included in a shared file.

To send a template, open New project, find the template under My templates, press Share, then press Make the file.

To receive a file, nobody needs a code. Open Prepared Pro, go to Backup and restore, choose the file and pick the merge option. The shared project or template is added to what you already have, and nothing is replaced.

When the other person finishes, they open the project, open the Go to menu and choose Send back to you. Prepared Pro makes a small file with only what they changed. They do not need a Team code to send changes back. They type their name once, so their changes carry it.

When you receive that file, open Backup and restore and choose it. A list opens showing each change: what it was, what it is now, and who made it. Tick the ones you want and press Add the ticked changes. If you changed the same thing yourself, it is not ticked and is marked so you can decide. Nothing is added until you say so.

Everything you add is marked "from Bob" with the date, on the checklist item or the outline section, and the project overview keeps a list of changes from your team. The mark clears if you later change that item or section yourself.

Sending needs a Team code. Press the License line, paste the code that starts with PT1 and press Activate. The line then reads Team edition unlocked. A Team code also unlocks everything in the full version.

The six kinds of project

  • Teaching and Talks: a sermon, lesson, talk or devotional.
  • Event and Keynote: a conference, banquet or any stage appearance, with travel and a bring list.
  • Writing and Publishing: an article, a book chapter or a whole book.
  • Class and Workshop: a series of sessions with handouts and materials.
  • Script, Film and Video: a script, a shoot, an episode or a video.
  • Goals and Objectives: a business plan, a year plan, team or office goals, organization objectives, a budget year, a grant plan or a personal target, with owners, measures and regular check-ins.

Each kind starts with a checklist that fits it. For video editing, choose Browse templates and pick Video editing and post, which brings review rounds and a list of deliverables. For software work, choose Browse templates and pick Software development in the Technology group, which brings ten checklist groups from the idea and requirements through design, setup, building, testing, release and support. You can add a Marketing checklist and a Publishing and rights checklist when you create a project, or later under Edit project.

Sources and research tools

Keep your references on the Sources page: books, articles, links and notes. The Research tools page holds the links you use most. Add your own, and they stay with you across projects.

Speaker view

Script. On the outline page, press Script to write or build one page of exactly what you will say. Open Build it from my notes, tick the parts of your outline and the Notebook notes you want, choose whether to include headlines, bullet points and talking notes, and press Add to the end of my script (or Replace). Then change it however you like. A line that starts with ## begins a new part, and the arrows in the teleprompter jump from part to part. The script can be up to 200,000 characters. When a project has a script, the teleprompter reads it, and a Show outline button lets you switch back. You can also print the script, make a Word document, dictate into it, and use the size and font lists on selected words. The script goes along with your backups and shared projects.

Press Scroll to start automatic scrolling, like a teleprompter, and use the minus and plus buttons to change its speed (1 is slowest, 10 is fastest). Tap the text, or press Pause, to stop for a moment, and tap again to go on. If you scroll by hand it carries on from where you left it. Press Look to choose the colors (including your own text and background colors), the font, the line spacing and bold text, to show a reading line, or to mirror the text for a teleprompter glass. Your choices are remembered on this device. Open Speaker view from the Go to menu or the Outline page. It shows one section at a time in large type, with a timer, so you can follow your outline while you speak. On supported devices it keeps the screen awake. Use the Next and Back buttons to move through your sections.

Cues: put a note to yourself in [square brackets] in your talking notes, for example [Ad read] or [Pause]. In Speaker view cues show in a different style so you can tell them from the words you say, and you can hide them in Look. They are left off printed pages and Word copies. If you open Speaker view for an episode in a series, a Next episode button appears and moves on to the following one by date, skipping any marked Delivered.

Things I Track: medicines, vehicles, home and pets

Press Things I Track in the left column to keep a list of your medications and supplements, your vehicles, things in your home and your pets. Each one can have up to 6 photos (a bottle label, a serial plate, a receipt, your pet), the doctor or vet, where you get it or where it is serviced (with the address, phone and web link), an order or refill link, dates to remember, and a record of what was done and when. A date can repeat; press Done and the next date is set for you. What is coming up shows on My Day. Doctors, hospitals, pharmacies, vets and shops are kept under Places, and you can add one while you add an item. For medicines and supplements, Print the medicine list for my doctor (or the Word file or Copy as text) makes one clear list with the dose, when you take it, who prescribed it and where you get it, without photos or web links. It stays on your device and is not medical advice. On My Day, the Find a Person or Place box finds a contact, doctor or shop as you type, with Call, Email, Text and Directions buttons, and the calendar is larger.

Timer

The Timer (in the Go to menu, and on the Shots and Call sheet pages) has two modes. Stopwatch counts up, and Mark take records each take with its own length. Countdown counts down from 1 to 30 minutes, or from minutes you enter, and sounds and vibrates when time is up. It keeps running when you close the window, and keeps the screen awake while it runs.

Updating Prepared Pro

Prepared Pro updates itself. When a newer version is published, your device picks it up the next time you open the app while connected to the internet.

  • To get it right away, open Help and about and press Check for updates now. It clears the saved copy and reloads, and your work stays on your device. Or close the app completely and open it again. On an iPad or iPhone, swipe it away first. If it still looks the same, reload the page once.
  • Your projects stay on your device through an update. Press Backup and restore now and then anyway. It takes a few seconds.
  • To see which version you have, look at the opening screen, where the version number is shown at the bottom.
  • Your projects live at the web address where you use the app. If you start using it at a different address, for example when the program moves to a new home, your projects do not follow by themselves. At the old address, press Backup and restore and save a backup. At the new address, open Backup and restore, choose that file and press Add to what I have.

Video editing, review rounds and deliverables

A Video Editing and Post project starts with a checklist for the whole job: intake, footage, the edit, sound and picture, review and delivery. The outline holds the edit as segments, with notes for each one, so you can write down timecodes and what to fix. Imported scripts and transcripts work here too.

  • Review rounds: keep each version you send, such as Cut 1 and Cut 2, with the date sent, when feedback is due, who is reviewing, a link or file name, and their notes. Tap the circle to move a round from Not sent to Sent, waiting to Approved.
  • Deliverables: list every file you must hand over, with its settings, where it goes and when it is due. Tap the circle to move each from To export to Exported to Checked and sent.
  • People, Gear and Schedule work as they do for filming. Feedback and delivery dates appear in Calendar and reminders.

On any other kind of project, press Choose extras (or Go to, then Add or remove extras) and switch on Editing and review rounds.

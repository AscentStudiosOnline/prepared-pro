Prepared Pro, stage 17 (series reorder)
=====================

What is in this folder
  index.html               The whole app (no other code needed)
  manifest.webmanifest     Lets it install to a Home Screen / desktop
  sw.js                    Makes it work offline after the first visit
  icons/                   App icons
  lib/                     Export tools (Word and PowerPoint). Upload this folder too.

To try it on one computer
  Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. It works straight from the file.
  Installing and offline use need the files on a real web address (see below).

To put it on your site (WordPress)
  1. Create a subdomain, for example prepared.ascentstudios.online, in your hosting control panel.
  2. Upload every file in this folder, keeping the icons folder, into that subdomain's folder.
  3. Open the address on an iPad: Share, then Add to Home Screen.
     On desktop Chrome or Edge: the install icon in the address bar.

Updating later
  When you upload a new index.html, open sw.js and change the number at the end of preparedpro-v3 (for example to preparedpro-v4)
  so everyone's copy refreshes.

Where your data lives
  Each person's projects are stored in their own browser on their own device.
  Use Backup and restore (bottom of the left column) to save a backup file and restore it on any device.

New in this version
  Series order: on the Series grid, each book has up and down arrows under its name. Move book 1 to the top, put
  book 7 last, whatever order you like. The order is saved with the series and in backups, and does not change your
  main project list. Books you add later go to the end.
  Outline counts toward readiness: each chapter or section counts as a required item worth the same as a
  medium checklist item (empty 0, some writing or in progress half, done full). The meter on the Overview gets an
  Outline segment you can click. It is on for new projects. Projects you made earlier stay as they were; switch
  it on or off any time in Edit project ("Count my outline in the readiness percentage"). A section with only a
  purpose filled in (for example one copied from a template) does not count as started.
  Idea prompts (built in, no AI, nothing sent anywhere): when a section is empty, tap "Get an idea to start", or
  in the edit box tap "Stuck? Get an idea". Pick what you are working on (openings, developing a point,
  illustrations, application, transitions, closing; for books chapter hooks, developing the chapter, endings and
  fact checks; for classes activities, discussion questions and ways to check understanding; for film scene
  openings, conflict, dialogue, pictures and sound). It starts on the right group for the section's name. Another
  idea cycles without repeating. Add to my notes puts the question in your notes for you to answer, with Undo.
  My templates: in a project, Go to menu, Save as template keeps its checklist (including any items you added or
  changed) and its chapter/section list, with an option to keep each section's purpose and word target. Your notes,
  bullets, headlines and big-picture answers are never saved in a template. When you make a new project, choose it
  under Start from. Manage my templates (in the New project box) renames or deletes them. Templates are included
  in backups.
  Speaker view (Go to menu, or the Outline page): a full-screen, distraction-free view of your outline to speak
  from. Big text with A- and A+, a choice of Both, Notes only or Bullets only, dark or light, previous and next
  section (buttons or arrow keys), "3 of 7" so you know where you are, and a timer that shows your planned time
  (from "How long you speak" in Event details, or estimated from your notes) and turns orange when you run over.
  The screen stays awake while it is open on devices that allow it. Sections with nothing written are skipped.
  Your text size, view and light/dark choice are remembered on that device.
  Calendar and reminders (left column): one list of what is coming up (delivery dates, events, flights, hotels,
  check-outs), grouped Overdue, This week and Later; tap one to open its project. Download a calendar file
  (.ics), choose what to include and when to be reminded (morning of, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week before), and open it
  on your phone or computer. Your own calendar app then sends the reminders, even when Prepared Pro is closed.
  The left column also shows how many things fall in the next week.
  Ask my AI: on any chapter or section (open it, then Ask my AI), or for the whole outline from the Outline page.
  Pick a task (tighten, stronger opening, examples, find gaps, slide bullets, draft notes, check tone), see exactly
  what will be copied, copy it, and paste it into your AI. Only that section and your big picture are copied.
  Paste the answer back to add it to the talking notes or replace the bullets, with Undo. Nothing is sent
  automatically. Every prompt tells the AI not to invent quotes, statistics, sources or Scripture references.
  Appearance (left column): Match my device, Light or Dark; text size Normal, Large, Larger, Largest;
  font Classic, Modern, Book or Easy to read; accent color Teal, Blue or Plum. Changes apply as you pick them
  and are remembered on that device. They are not part of backups, so each device keeps its own look.
  Automatic stage: the stage (Idea, Research, Outline, Draft, Review) follows your checklist progress:
  under 25% is Research, 25-49% Outline, 50-74% Draft, 75% and up Review. It moves back if you reopen items.
  Delivered is the one you set yourself ("mark it delivered", with Reopen to undo).
  Rights and permissions checklist: tick it when you make a project, or later from Edit project. Books get
  copyright, ISBN, barcode, quote and Scripture permissions and cover rights. Film gets script registration,
  music and footage licenses, releases and title clearance. Talks and classes get quote, image, music and
  recording permissions. Remove it any time (you are asked first if you have ticked anything).
  License code: the free version allows 2 projects. A license code (from your receipt) unlocks unlimited
  projects. Codes are checked inside the app with no internet needed. Nothing is ever locked or deleted
  when someone is over the limit. To point the Buy button at your Podia page, open index.html, find
  BUY_URL near the top of the script and replace the address. Make codes with Prepared Pro Code Maker.html (kept private).
  Export or print (Go to menu, or the Outline page): Word document, PowerPoint slides, Print or save as PDF,
  or copy as text. Choose what to include and a look (Clean, Classic, Bold). PowerPoint puts your talking
  notes in the speaker notes. Books export to Word only.
  Event and travel: date, place, host, equipment, flights, rides and hotel, with a copyable trip sheet.
  Bring list: equipment and materials with To get, Ready and Packed, starter lists and your usual list.
  Backup and restore: save everything to a file and bring it back, or add it to what you have.
  Switch on Event and travel or Bring list per project from the Go to menu, Add or remove extras.
  Series: group several books or projects, see them on one grid, tick your own milestones
  (Written, Edited, Published, Commercial made, Social media live, Audiobook) and keep links
  (Amazon, Audible, your site) for each. Open it from the Series button in the left column.
  Simpler screens: a project opens to an Overview with one next step. A Go to menu reaches the
  Outline, Checklist and any extras. Sources and Research tools are extras you switch on per project.
  Checklist items can hold notes, and a Write button jumps to where the content belongs.
  Outline: the real document. Each project has a big-picture card and sections with a headline,
  purpose, subject matter, bullets, talking notes and sources. Books use chapters, films use scenes.
  Completed view: filter the checklist by All, Still missing or Done. Done items show the date,
  can be reopened, and an Undo appears after you mark one done.

Earlier
  Sources: save each book, site or person per project, then copy a bibliography.
  Research tools: save links to your AI assistant and other sites; Copy for my AI
  puts the project and what is missing on your clipboard to paste into your AI.
